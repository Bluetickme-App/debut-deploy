Construction and spacing

Clear space

Keep clear space equal to half the hexagon width on all sides. Nothing — type, rules, edges or imagery — enters that area.

Minimum sizes

Below 24px the diagonal traces close up — use the favicon variant, which keeps only the horizontal pair.

On dark surfaces

Use the white outline mark or the white lockup. Do not place the gradient mark on the near-black surface — the blue loses separation against it.
