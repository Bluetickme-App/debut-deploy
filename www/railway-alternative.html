A fixed monthly box instead of a usage meter.

The short version

Predictability

A price you can budget

No vCPU-minute or GB-hour arithmetic. Choose CPU and memory, pay that amount monthly, and read the invoice before the month rather than after it.

Egress

20 TB included vs per-GB billing

Railway charges for outbound transfer per gigabyte. On media-heavy or API-heavy apps this is frequently the largest single line.

Seats

Seats are free here

Railway bills per member on Pro. Every DebutDeploy plan includes unlimited team members.
