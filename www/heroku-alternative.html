The short version

Price

Dyno pricing has not aged well

Heroku charges premium rates for modest memory. The same 4 GB of RAM costs a small fraction here.

Memory

No 512 MB ceiling

Standard dynos cap at 1 GB before a large jump to Performance tiers. Our plans step 512 MB → 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 GB at linear prices.

Database

Postgres is not an add-on tax

Managed Postgres starts at $5/mo with nightly backups included rather than being priced as a separate premium product.
