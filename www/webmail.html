How a password reset works

Four steps, about a minute.

  1. Ask for a link. Go to the reset page and enter your email address.
  2. Check your backup inbox. The link goes to the backup address registered for your mailbox — not to the mailbox itself, since you can't get into that one.
  3. Choose a new password. The link works once and expires after an hour. Asking again replaces any earlier link.
  4. Sign back in. Use the new password in webmail and update it in any mail app or phone you've set up.

No backup address on file?

The reset only works if your mailbox has one — it's where the link is sent. Whoever administers your email sets it in the DebutDeploy panel, and they can also set a new password for you directly. If you're not sure who that is, ask the person who gave you the mailbox, or contact support@debutdepoly.com.

For your own safety, the reset page gives the same answer whether or not an address is registered with us — so nobody can use it to work out which mailboxes exist.
